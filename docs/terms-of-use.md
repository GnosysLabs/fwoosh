## 1. What Fwoosh does

Fwoosh is a desktop and mobile application for synchronizing user-selected clipboard text and transferring user-selected files between paired devices. It uses end-to-end encryption for supported content paths, an ntfy-compatible relay for encrypted messages and signaling, and peer-to-peer networking for file transfers.

Depending on your configuration, the relay may be public ntfy.sh, a customer-selected or self-hosted ntfy server, or a Fwoosh-operated relay offered under a Pro or other paid plan. Fwoosh does not provide a general-purpose plaintext cloud storage service, guaranteed message delivery, or a managed backup service.
